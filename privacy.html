Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · applies to Tumbledown! version 1.0 for iPhone and to this website

The short version

Tumbledown! does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game works fully offline and your progress stays on your iPhone. We never sell or share data, because we never have any.

1. Who we are

Tumbledown! is made by Mohannad Abdelkarim, an independent game developer based in Egypt ("we", "us"). You can reach us at tumbledown.support@gmail.com.

2. What the app keeps on your iPhone

To remember your game, the app saves a small file in its own storage on your device. It contains:

  • your progress: the level you are on, your stars, coins, lives and the life timer, rockets, and the balls, items and cards you have unlocked or seen;
  • your Daily Shot results and streak, your festival builds, and your event tokens and prizes;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, events, ball);
  • the dates on which the game last asked iOS to show the App Store rating prompt, so it does not ask too often.

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include it, as they include other app data; Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

3. What we do not collect

The app does not collect, send or store on any server: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, device or advertising identifiers (such as the IDFA), IP address, usage statistics, crash reports or any other information. The app makes no network connections at all and contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics or crash reporting. It never asks for permission to track you.

4. Apple features the app uses

  • Ratings. A few times a year at most, the game asks iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple decides whether it appears. Any rating or review you leave goes to Apple and the App Store.
  • Sharing. The Daily Shot's Share button opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text (for example "Tumbledown Daily #12 ⭐⭐⭐ 9/20 balls"). You choose where it goes; we do not receive it.
  • Links. The Privacy Policy and Support buttons in Settings open this website in Safari.
  • Apple's own data. Apple processes App Store downloads and, if you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, may give us anonymous crash reports and aggregated statistics. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and we use it only to fix problems.

5. This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses to run and protect its service, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Children

Tumbledown! is a game for a general audience. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you, we do not share them, and we keep them only as long as needed to help you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

8. Your rights

Because the app sends us no personal data, we hold nothing about you from the game that we could show, correct, export or delete; the game data is under your control on your device. For emails you sent us, you can ask us to see or delete them. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Future versions

If a future update adds something that handles personal data (for example advertising or in-app purchases), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before that update is released, and the app will ask for your permission wherever the law or Apple requires it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page and update the date at the top.
